What the divider needs, read off the pseudocode in "Lab2 practice.docx".

REGISTERS  (rule 5, one per name on the left of a transfer)

  A               4 bits   dividend, flipped positive in s2
  B               4 bits   divisor, flipped positive in s3
  S               8 bits   S(7..4) remainder, S(3..0) quotient
                           needs: load high, load low, shift left,
                           and a way to write S(0) on its own in s7 and s8
  i               3 bits   counts 4 passes, decremented in s9
  SA, SB          1 bit    the two signs, captured in s1 before the flips
  Divide_by_zero  1 bit    set in s4

DEVICES

  adderSubtractor   the 4 bit one from the pre-lab. Every piece of arithmetic
                    here is 4 bits: the negates in s2, s3, s10 and s11, and
                    the subtract in s7. No 8 bit unit needed, unlike the
                    multiplier.
  comparator4       for c3, S(7..4) >= B.  Built from comparator1.
  zero detect on B  for c2.  A NOR across the four bits.
  zero detect on i  for c4, i > 1.
  multiplexers      wherever two paths reach one register input, by rule 7.

TWO THINGS TO SETTLE BEFORE WRITING THE DATAPATH

  c5 in the pseudocode is a condition box that both branches leave without
  doing anything, and s10 is reached from it as well as from c4. Check the
  transitions there against the chart.

  Dividing the most negative value by minus one gives a quotient that does not
  fit in four signed bits. Decide what the circuit does in that case.
